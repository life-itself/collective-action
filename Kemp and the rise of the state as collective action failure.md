# Kemp and the rise of the state as collective action failure

How the early state can be read as a collective action failure rather than a gain in cooperation: concentrated power and arms races emerged because each actor's best response to aggressive neighbors was to match them.

## Kemp

- [[Kemp on early elites and arms races]] — a few aggressive rulers set the incentives for everyone nearby; the 'race to the bottom'
- [[Kemp on conflict and in-group cooperation]] — external threat raises cooperation inside the group and hostility outside it
- [[Bauer et al on war and cooperation]] — the empirical review Kemp relies on for the second note
