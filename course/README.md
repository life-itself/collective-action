Why cooperation fails, how it succeeds, and how it scales: from arms races and the commons to AI, climate and the metacrisis. These are the chapter pages of the course, led by Rufus Pollock. Each page holds the full argument, with the images, clips, footnotes and questions to think about; the live sessions cover less, so the pages are the place to catch up or go deeper.

The course runs as a ladder. First, a portable tool for diagnosing collective action problems. Then a climb through the scales at which humans have made cooperation work, from small groups to tribes to states. Along the way we test a claim: that culture leads each jump in scale (not always, not alone), and what that says about cooperating as a planet.
