# Recommended shortlist

Cut from the [176 candidates](index.md). The full lists stay as a research
database — this is what to actually use, and each entry says what job it does.

## Criteria

Scored on, in rough weight order:

1. **Teaches a mechanism, not an outcome.** About two-thirds of the candidates
   are outcomes. "Everyone overfished and the fish died" is an anecdote;
   "the wintering rule replaces an unmeasurable quantity with one every
   neighbour verifies for free" is a move you can reuse.
2. **Structural distinctness.** The set must span prisoner's dilemma,
   coordination and assurance, subtractable commons, positive commons,
   threshold goods and arms races.
3. **Is it felt.** A case has to land before the formalism means anything.
4. **Scale distinctness**, and pairing across scales.
5. **Evidential safety.** A contested case costs two minutes of caveat with
   this audience, worth paying only for a uniquely good mechanism.
6. **Cuts against priors.** This audience knows Moloch and knows the tragedy of
   the commons. Overturning an assumption beats confirming one.
7. **Reuse across modules**, and **balance** — the course is about opportunities
   as much as failures.

## The spine — six that carry the course

**If a Tree Falls** *(already chosen, prep viewing)* — activists who solved a
hard cooperation problem for years, then informed on each other within weeks of
arrest. S1.

**Bank runs and Diamond–Dybvig** — the purest coordination failure, structurally
*unlike* a prisoner's dilemma: two equilibria, and which one you get depends only
on what each depositor expects everyone else to expect. Deposit insurance fixes
it by deleting an equilibrium, not by improving anyone's character. Carries the
dilemma/coordination/assurance distinction, which is the one that does real work.
Photographs, and a 90-second film scene that explains the whole mechanism.
S2, S4.

**Törbel's wintering rule** — no more cows on the alp than you can winter on your
own land. Counting grass eaten on a shared alp is impossible; counting a
neighbour's hay barn in February is free, and everyone in a village of 600 does
it as a side effect of living there. Observability engineered into the rule
itself. The best single mechanism in the whole set, and uncontested. S3.

**Montreal / Kigali / leaded petrol / CFC-11** — one slot, four cases, run as a
controlled comparison rather than a success story. Montreal worked on an easy
problem (few producers, cheap substitutes, a trade ban that bit). Kigali is a
*climate* agreement that worked, because it was shaped like an ozone agreement —
which kills "climate is intrinsically harder". Leaded petrol was phased out
globally with no treaty at all, isolating substitutes as the operative variable.
CFC-11 is observability tested live: the violation was caught from the
atmosphere, not from anyone's reporting, and stopped within two years. S4.

**Fork-tailed drongo false alarm calls** — a bird that free-rides on an alarm
system's credibility, and escalates to mimicking meerkat calls once its own stop
being believed. An arms race, in a bird, on film. Best-filmed cheating case that
exists. S1, S3.

**Dictyostelium cheater strains** — a free rider with no cognition, no intention,
no culture and no morality. The strongest available support for the course's
central claim that defection is not a deficiency, because there is nobody there
to be deficient. Independently evolved again in *Myxococcus*, which makes the
point twice. S2.

## High value, distinct jobs

**The trench truces** — repeated play with the diagnostic running visibly:
cooperation appeared where units faced each other for months and could observe
each other, and died the moment command rotated units and forced raids. Axelrod
already treats it, so theory and case arrive together. S3.

**The Great Stink (1858)** — sanitation went unprovided for twenty years while
everyone agreed it was needed, and was funded in about three weeks once the smell
reached Parliament. A failure that got *solved*, and the cleanest statement that
nobody was a villain — the cost simply sat in the wrong place. Public domain
cartoons. S1 opener candidate.

**Philosophical Transactions and the invention of priority (1665)** — dated
publication turned giving a discovery away into the way to *claim* it. A
selective incentive bolted onto a public good, 300 years before Olson named the
move. Fills the undersupply half of the commons distinction, which is otherwise
nearly absent from the whole candidate set. S3.

**Anglo-German naval race + the Washington Naval Treaty** — as a pair. The
textbook arms race, and then the same structure actually solved, with a
countable, observable unit as the mechanism. S1, S4.

**The frontier AI race** — the audience's live case, and distinctive because
participants state the trap openly in their own founding documents. S1.

**The Limited Test Ban Treaty (1963)** — the scope of the agreement was set by
what could be verified unilaterally. Atmospheric tests leave fallout; underground
tests didn't show up, so they were left out. Detection technology determined what
could be agreed, not trust. Pairs directly with Schelling. S4.

**Meerkat sentinels** — recommended *because the standard telling is wrong*.
Guards are well-fed, in a safe spot, first to flee, with zero deaths in 2,000
hours of observation. A lesson in how readily we read altruism into a structure
that doesn't need it. S3.

**Balinese subak and the water temples** — coordination rather than dilemma, with
ritual as the synchronisation device. The bridge case: it belongs to Ostrom's
material and prefigures session 5's argument that ritual is a cooperation
technology. Lansing's account is contested (Helmreich); the case stands, the
strength of the mechanism is what's disputed. S3, S5.

## Mix and balance

**Orca wave-washing** *(or musk oxen)* — a threshold public good that cannot be
produced alone, and a wordless opening image. Orca has the better footage; musk
oxen make the threshold point more sharply, since a half-formed ring is worse
than none. S1, S3.

**Los Angeles groundwater basins** — Ostrom's own dissertation and the empirical
root of polycentricity, which is otherwise the most abstract module in session 4.
Hundreds of independent pumpers, nested rather than centralised. S4.

**Wikipedia and the open source maintainer problem** — the same positive-commons
structure, one working and one failing. Every firm depends on the infrastructure,
none pays. The audience lives inside this one. S3, S4.

**The liberum veto** — a unanimity rule adopted for good reasons, which destroyed
a state. Institutional design as the cause rather than human nature, which is a
useful corrective in a course that could otherwise read as being about character.
S2, S4.

**The Danish rescue, October 1943** — the course needs at least one case of
people doing the right thing at real risk, without organisation or leadership.
Worth keeping the honest caveats attached (light occupation, paid passage). S5.

## Deliberately not recommended

**Rapa Nui, Norse Greenland, the Ik, the Donner Party.** The four most
memorable-sounding cases in the whole set, and the four weakest evidentially.
Rapa Nui and Norse Greenland are exactly the Moloch-flavoured collapse narratives
the course intends to argue with — usable only as case studies in how collapse
stories get selected.

**The commons-depletion long tail** — cod, anchoveta, bison, passenger pigeon,
Aral Sea, Dust Bowl. Six cases teaching one lesson. Pick one, probably cod for
recency or bison for the images, and keep the rest in the database.

**Kyoto on its own.** Only teaches inside the controlled comparison above.

## Additions from the warfare and fiction hunts

Both gaps are now filled, and the material is strong enough to displace some of
the above. These are the promotions.

### Warfare — the mechanisms of cohesion

**The syssitia** (Spartan common messes) — three cheap mechanisms stacked. Daily
compulsory attendance makes absence instantly observable to the fifteen men who
will stand beside you in the line. A fixed food contribution is a recurring
costly signal, with loss of citizenship as the penalty for failing it. Admission
is by unanimous ballot, so every member has been vouched for individually by
every other. This is the direct answer to "what did it take to make hoplites".
It also carries its own bill — the quota required land, and land required
helots, which feeds S5. **S3, S5.**

**The tresantes** (Spartan "tremblers") — the sharpest case in the entire set for
a problem nothing else covers: *who punishes the punishers*. A death penalty has
to be administered by someone and is over quickly. Social death is administered
by everyone, at zero cost, continuously, by exactly the people whose regard the
sanction runs on. Plutarch's report that Agesilaus let the laws "sleep for a day"
after Leuctra is the tell that it only worked while the numbers were small.
**S3.**

**Decimation and the fustuarium** — two mechanisms usually conflated. The
*fustuarium* conscripts the punishers: the condemned man's own comrades do the
killing, so enforcement costs the state nothing and implicates them in it.
Decimation deliberately severs guilt from punishment by drawing lots, and what
that buys is collective liability — since you may die for your neighbour's
flight, you now have a reason to stop him running. **S3, S4.**

**The hoplite shield wall** — each man's shield partly covers the man to his
left, so the line is a public good that collapses if anyone runs: mutual
hostage-taking plus total observability. Thucydides (5.71) on the rightward drift
of advancing phalanxes is the evidence that individual incentives pull against
the formation even while it holds. **S1, S3.**

**The Plataean breakout** (Thucydides 3.20-24) — the unexpected find. Counting
bricks in the enemy wall by having many men count independently is aggregation
substituting for measurement. Roughly half the men who agreed to go lost their
nerve at the moment of escape — and because they did, the escape got *safer* for
those who went. Free riding that helps is rare enough to be worth a slide.
**S1, S3.**

**Krentz's casualty ratio** — losers roughly 14%, winners roughly 5%, in Greek
hoplite battle. The quantitative statement of why the line holds: almost all the
dying happens after it breaks. Pairs with Caporetto for the same phenomenon two
millennia later. **S3.**

### Fiction — clips that stand alone

**Dr. Strangelove**, the War Room doomsday-machine exchange (~5 min) — the
characters explain the theory inside the scene: a commitment device must be
automatic *and* announced, and a secret one is worthless. Almost certainly the
highest-value clip in the course for the Schelling module, which is the module
the AI-interested part of the audience is coming for. **S4.**

**Norma Rae**, the UNION sign (~3 min) — a threshold cascade rendered almost
without dialogue, in sound, as the machines shut off one by one. The cleanest
*success* available as a clip, and S1 needs one early. **S1, S3.**

**Twelve Angry Men**, the secret ballot (~4 min) — a mechanism deliberately
designed to break an information cascade, shown working. **S2, S3.**

**Battleship Potemkin**, the firing squad — silent, public domain, and it plays
to an audience that has never heard of 1905. **S1.**

### What these displace

The list is now around 24, which is too many. I'd cut **the liberum veto** (the
institutional-design point is made better by decimation, where the rule
*creates* the incentive rather than destroying one), **the Danish rescue**
(Norma Rae carries "people acted at real risk" better as a clip, and with a
mechanism attached), and one of **Anglo-German / Washington Naval** — keep the
pair only if S4 has room, since Dr. Strangelove now does the commitment-device
work more vividly.

That lands at 20.

## Notes on the fiction list

Four entries — Thucydides, Xenophon, *Things Fall Apart*, *The Plague* — are
marked prep reading rather than in-session material, since no usable footage
exists. Weak fits are flagged as weak: *Jaws* has no defection in it and is
better used as the shape of a climate argument than as a game; *The Dark Knight*
ferries is closer to mutual deterrence with a deadline than a strict prisoner's
dilemma. Two structures came back genuinely thin — commons collapse as plot
(fiction wants a villain and a commons failure has none, so documentary serves
better) and coordination without communication.

## Expanding any of these

Each entry names its structure, so "more like this one" is answerable from the
full lists: more observability-by-design → the zanjeras, Alanya rotation, iriai
synchronised harvesting. More positive commons → Republic of Letters, IGY
mirrored archives, the UPU. More arms races → July Crisis, New START expiry,
NPT. More cheating-in-nature → cleaner wrasse, *Pseudomonas*, acacia parasite.
