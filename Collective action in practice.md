# Collective action in practice

The applied record. Most deliberate attempts at large-scale cooperation have failed; a few have worked, and the differences between them are legible.

## Worked, partly worked, failed

- [[Montreal Protocol]] — the clearest success, and an unusually easy case
- [[Kyoto Protocol]] — the clearest failure of the same era
- [[Nuclear non-proliferation]] — partial, and still holding
- [[Fisheries collapse]]

## What the difference turns on

Roughly the five questions of [[The collective action diagnostic]]: how many players, whether defection is observable, whether it can be punished, whether the players meet again, and whether anyone is large enough to act alone. Substitutes matter too — Montreal had them and Kyoto didn't.
