# Governing the commons

The tragedy of the commons is a prediction that real communities frequently falsify. This is the empirical literature on when and how they manage it.

## Kinds of commons

Not one problem but two, with different failure modes and different fixes.

- [[Subtractable commons]] — overuse and depletion: grazing, fisheries, groundwater
- [[Information commons]] — [[Undersupply]] rather than overuse
