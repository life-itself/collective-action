## Things to know before working here

- **Voice is deferred** for wiki notes and research. Write plainly; a systematic
  sweep happens later (`moloch-gpl.7`). Voice still applies to course-facing
  material — the landing page, scripts, decks.
- **Excerpts are the binding requirement instead.** Every note carries at least
  one attributed direct quote as a blockquote. Keep them short and precise, and
  never quote a source you haven't read.
- **Each bead names its model** — `agent:sonnet`, `agent:opus`, `agent:human`.
  See [docs/delegation.md](docs/delegation.md).
- `bd ready` shows what's unblocked. `bd show <id>` gives a bead's acceptance
  criteria, which say what "done" means.
