An X post by @IterIntellectus shares a motion-design video titled “PROMETHEUS II.” It moves through seventeen chapters, beginning with Athens and ending at a Dyson sphere, with the final word “ACCELERATE.” The post says the author asked Claude to make a video about Western civilization; the post itself does not establish how the video was produced.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AUznMzAACmU

The video is an example of a classic [[Ascent of civilization narrative]]: history is organized as a sequence of accumulated achievements leading toward greater technical capacity. Its arc gives technological progress a direction and destination. That framing can obscure discontinuities, costs, and alternative measures of progress.
